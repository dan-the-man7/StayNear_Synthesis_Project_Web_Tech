Find a hostel you can walk to class from

Every semester, students pay for rooms they have never seen. StayNear lets you compare hostels side by side and book a viewing before you pay anything.

Compare hostels

How it works

  1. Compare prices and facilities on the comparison table.
  2. Pick a hostel and fill in the viewing form.
  3. Visit, ask questions, then decide.

What to check on a viewing

  • Water and power
    • Is there a storage tank?
    • Is the meter prepaid or shared?
  • Security at night
  • Walking time to the lecture halls

Words landlords use

Caution fee
A refundable deposit against damage.
Prepaid meter
You buy electricity credit before you use it.
Self-contained
The room has its own bathroom and kitchenette.

Safety tip

Never pay a deposit before a viewing. Take a friend with you.
